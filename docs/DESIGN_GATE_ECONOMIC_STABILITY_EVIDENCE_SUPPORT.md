# Design Gate — Economic Stability Evidence Support

**Status:** APPROVED — V1 bounded consumer of economic health and stability evidence.

## Purpose

Expose economic profitability and stability evidence together without collapsing them into a universal score or automatic portfolio posture.

## V1

Inputs:
- EconomicHealthEvidence
- EconomicStabilityAssessment

Outputs:
- profitability rate
- average actual profit
- profit variability ratio when available
- explicit stability eligibility
- preserved business/window/outcome lineage

Rules:
1. Health metrics remain descriptive evidence.
2. Stability eligibility remains policy-derived.
3. No universal economic-health score is produced.
4. No portfolio posture, capital allocation, forecasting, policy mutation, or financial execution is produced.
5. Assessment eligibility does not rewrite the underlying health evidence.
6. Business and window identity must match.
7. Missing variability remains explicit; it is never treated as zero.
